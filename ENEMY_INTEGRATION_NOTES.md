# Enemy artwork integration (updated)

The old generated enemy portraits were replaced with team-authored Aseprite
artwork. For the complete path mapping, emotions, and workflow, see
[CHARACTER_ART_INTEGRATION.md](CHARACTER_ART_INTEGRATION.md).

Every topic enemy has **idle**, **correct**, and **hurt** sprites shared across
Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced. The difficulty settings remain independent
of the artwork.
